# Artwork

Trees, palms, ferns, flowers, rocks, boat, explorer, birds, terrain, water, pickups, buildings, and interface are project-authored procedural artwork. No photographic textures or external 3D models are needed. The user’s references guide the illustrated palette and silhouettes; reference images are not included in the game.

`models/perched-gull.glb` is the project-authored stationary reference study. Its continuous silhouette and feather surfaces are authored in `src/assets/perched-gull.ts` and exported with `scripts/export-perched-gull.ts`. Plumage is vertex painted; no image textures or third-party model files are embedded. This model is for shape review before rigging and animation.
