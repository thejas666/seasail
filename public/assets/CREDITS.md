# Nature assets

Fern 02 and Rock 07 by Poly Haven, used under CC0:

- https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02
- https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_07
- https://polyhaven.com/license

The bundled GLB files were resized, simplified, and compressed with glTF Transform. Trees, palms, boat, explorer, birds, terrain, water, and interface are project-authored procedural artwork. The illustrated tree references guide the palette and silhouettes; no reference image is included in the game.
