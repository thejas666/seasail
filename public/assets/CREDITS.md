# Artwork

Trees, palms, ferns, flowers, rocks, boat, explorer, birds, terrain, water, pickups, buildings, and interface are project-authored procedural artwork. No photographic textures or external 3D models are needed. The user’s references guide the illustrated palette and silhouettes; reference images are not included in the game.

`models/perched-gull.glb` is the project-authored stationary reference study. Its continuous silhouette and feather surfaces are authored in `src/assets/perched-gull.ts` and exported with `scripts/export-perched-gull.ts`. The game uses those same surfaces through the rig in `src/assets/animated-gull.ts`. Plumage is vertex painted; no image textures or third-party model files are embedded. The standalone GLB remains static, while runtime bones and morph targets drive the game animations. Bird anatomy and motion were informed by RSPB and Cornell gull references and Harvey and Inman's 2022 study of gull wing morphing; their images and research assets are not bundled.

The `palm-*.glb`, `moss-rock.glb`, `sandstone.glb`, and `coastal-*.glb` models are project-authored from the user's illustrated reference sheet. Curved trunks, notched fronds, bark rings, moss patches, stone strata, and foliage are modeled and vertex painted in `src/assets/palm-tree.ts`, `src/assets/coastal-nature.ts`, and `src/assets/grass-tuft.ts`. `scripts/export-coastal-assets.ts` generates the standalone models. No reference image or external texture is embedded.
