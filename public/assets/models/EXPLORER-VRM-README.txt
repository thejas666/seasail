explorer.vrm is the character used by the Anime art style.

The bundled file is "VRM1_Constraint_Twist_Sample" v1.0.1, (c) 2022 pixiv Inc., from the three-vrm
examples (https://github.com/pixiv/three-vrm). Its VRM licence (https://vrm.dev/licenses/1.0/) allows
redistribution, modification and commercial use, with no credit required.

To use your own character, make one in VRoid Studio (free), export it as VRM, name it explorer.vrm and
replace this file. If the file is missing or fails to load, the game falls back to its built-in character.

explorer-male.vrm is "AvatarSample_C" by VRoid (pixiv), one of the VRoid Studio sample avatars. Its VRM
metadata allows use by everyone, including commercial use; pixiv publishes the sample avatars as free to
use. Confirm the current terms on VRoid Hub before a commercial release, or replace it with your own model.
